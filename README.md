# Nice Bean Cafe – Website Copy Builder

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Nice Bean Cafe Website Copy Builder** is a prompt-based content generation project designed to create professional, attractive, and customer-friendly website content for a cafe. It generates content for the homepage, about us page, services page, contact page, and customer testimonials.

The content uses a friendly, modern, and attractive tone to appeal to students, working professionals, and other coffee lovers.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Generate professional website content for a cafe.
- Create engaging headlines and introductions.
- Highlight cafe benefits and special offerings.
- Present services and menu items clearly.
- Provide contact information and business hours.
- Generate short, friendly customer testimonials.

## 📄 Website Pages

### 1. Home Page
- Attractive headline and introduction.
- Benefits of visiting Nice Bean Cafe.
- Reasons to choose the cafe.
- Engaging call to action for customers.

### 2. About Us Page
- Company introduction.
- Cafe experience and background.
- Email and mobile number.
- Business address.

### 3. Services Page

**Coffee and Beverages**
- Coffee
- Tea
- Cappuccino
- Cinnamon tea
- Cold coffee

**Snacks**
- Palm cake
- Biscuits
- Cakes

**Sunday Special Menu**
- Special coffee
- Special cake

Each service includes a short description to help customers understand the available products and their benefits.

### 4. Contact Page
- Cafe contact details.
- Booking information.
- Customer enquiries.
- Business address.
- Business hours.
- Email address.

### 5. Customer Testimonials
The project generates three short, friendly, and professional customer testimonials, each limited to one line.

## 👥 Target Audience

- Students looking for a comfortable place to relax or study.
- Working professionals seeking coffee breaks.
- Friends and families looking for refreshments.
- Coffee, tea, and snack lovers.

## ✨ Key Features

- Professional website copy generation.
- Friendly and modern writing style.
- Clear descriptions of cafe products and services.
- Attractive calls to action.
- Dedicated content for each website page.
- Short and engaging customer testimonials.
- Content suitable for a cafe website.

## 🛠️ Technologies and Tools

- **AI Prompt Engineering:** For generating structured website content.
- **Natural Language Generation:** For creating readable and engaging copy.
- **Markdown:** For documenting project information.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Copy the provided Nice Bean Cafe prompt.
2. Paste it into an AI writing tool.
3. Generate content for each requested website page.
4. Review and customize the generated content.
5. Replace placeholder contact details with the cafe's actual information.
6. Add the content to the corresponding website pages.

## 📌 Expected Outcome

The project produces complete, professional, and customer-focused website copy for Nice Bean Cafe, helping present its menu, services, brand identity, and contact information in an appealing way.

## 📝 Note

The cafe's actual email address, mobile number, physical address, business hours, and experience details should be added before publishing. Testimonials generated by AI should be treated as sample content unless they reflect genuine customer feedback.
